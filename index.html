How to play

You start with 1,000 chips. Each round names a place anywhere on Earth and sets a line: tap within that distance to win.

Stack your chips (10 minimum), then tap the globe. Hit and you win your bet × the payout; miss and you lose it. Tight lines and obscure places pay more.

7 rounds a day, the same places for everyone. Go broke and you're out; cash out any time to lock in what you have. Try to finish with more than you started.

🔥 Hot streak: hit 3 in a row and your payouts get +0.5× until you miss. ⭐ Bonus round: the last place pays double.

Drag to spin the globe, pinch or scroll to zoom. It's a terrain map with country borders: no labels or roads.
